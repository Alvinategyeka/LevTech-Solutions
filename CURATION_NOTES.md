# Editorial curation notes

## The decision

The final edition uses **31 landscape pages**, not all 44. The goal is a single coherent LevTech publication rather than a page-for-page reproduction of the reference catalogue. The strongest LevTech-branded artwork was kept as the visual base, then service-specific pages were arranged into a clear customer journey.

## Narrative and page order

1. Cover — establishes the brand and promise.
2. Company profile — explains the company and positioning.
3. Our solutions — puts the entire portfolio into context.
4. Product-category directory — lets readers scan the smart-home device families.
5–8. CCTV and recording, solar/4G surveillance, and GPS/fleet visibility.
9–16. Access control and attendance, digital locks, intercoms and doorbells, intrusion and fire alarms, fencing, and gate automation.
17–27. Smart-home automation and controls, switches and sockets, smart plugs, lighting, sensors, climate, window treatments and switchable film, hubs/gateways, smart living devices, and voice assistants/audio.
28–30. Backup power, solar street lighting, networking and IP telephony.
31. Closing spread — integrated delivery, site assessment, installation, commissioning and support.

## Repeated material removed or consolidated

- CCTV surveillance has one lead page, supported by separate solar/4G camera and recorder/storage pages; the second general CCTV variant was not given another full spread.
- Similar digital-lock variants are consolidated into one digital-lock/keyless-entry section; smart furniture/cabinet locks remain represented in the broader smart-living section.
- Access control and attendance have one lead page; a near-duplicate access-control/RFID page was removed.
- Smart switches, dimmers, sockets and modules are treated together, while smart plugs/appliance control remain separate because they represent a distinct use case.
- Similar smart-sensor pages were consolidated into one environmental-monitoring/sensors page.
- Electric fencing and gate automation each have one definitive spread; repeated fencing and gate-motor variants are removed.
- Related smart-home control panel and hub/gateway pages remain distinct: the former is the user interface, while the latter addresses connectivity and ecosystems.
- Curtains/blinds and switchable film share a spread to avoid fragmenting a smaller category.

## Added coverage where the 44-page image sequence needed it

- GPS tracking and fleet management receives a dedicated page instead of being buried in a general overview.
- Networking and IP telephony receives a dedicated page because these are LevTech services in their own right.
- The final spread names the complete service groups and calls customers to request a site assessment/quotation.

## Visual direction

The catalogue is intentionally landscape and editorial: high-contrast navy/blue, white space, a consistent folio system, lifestyle imagery for context and product galleries for choice. Source pages with substantially duplicated content are not repeated simply to preserve a page count. The 44-page review previews are included in the archive for traceability, not as additional pages in the final catalogue.
